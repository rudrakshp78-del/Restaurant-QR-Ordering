Table QR Standees & Barcode Cards

Print and place on restaurant tables. Guests scan via Google Lens or Camera to order.

Customer Menu
How Google Lens / Phone Camera QR Scanning Works:

Each card below has a unique QR code generated for that specific table. When a guest points their smartphone camera or Google Lens at the barcode, their phone browser immediately opens the restaurant menu with that table pre-selected (e.g. http://10.190.32.46:3000/?table=1).
